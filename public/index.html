Quick Entry

Writing guide

Use natural text. Include date, item, quantity, and price when you know them.

Basic expense

bought sacks 300 pesos

Quantity and price

bought 25 sacks of charcoal for 300 pesos

Income

sold 10 trays eggs 220 each

Date words

paid helper yesterday 500 pesos

Bisaya / Tagalog

gipalit ug kahoy pang repair 400

Receivable payment

customer paid balance 1000

Good patterns

  • Item + total: bought medicine 350 pesos
  • Qty + unit + price: bought 2 bags feed 1600 each
  • Approximate: bought repair materials mga 400 pesos
  • Dates: gahapon, last Monday, May 14, 05/14/2026
